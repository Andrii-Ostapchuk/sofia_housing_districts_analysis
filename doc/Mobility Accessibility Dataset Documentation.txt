* **object_id** / **objectid**: Internal numeric identifiers for the specific row or feature within the geospatial database.


* **facilityid**: A unique numeric code identifying the physical mass transit stop.


* **name_**: The official name of the transit stop, appended with the walking distance interval that this specific row represents (e.g., "0 - 400" or "600 - 800" meters).


* **frombreak**: The starting distance of the walking catchment zone in meters. For example, a value of `0.0` represents the immediate area around the stop, while `600.0` represents a zone starting 600 meters away.


* **tobreak**: The maximum outward walking distance for this specific zone in meters. A value of `400.0` represents a roughly 5-minute walk, while `800.0` represents a roughly 10-minute walk limit.


* **id**: A universally unique identifier (UUID) assigned to the specific geospatial polygon.


* **area_kv_m**: The calculated physical area of this specific walking zone interval, measured in square meters.


* **geometry**: The spatial coordinates (`MultiPolygon`) that map the actual physical footprint of where a pedestrian can reach within the defined `frombreak` and `tobreak` distances along the street network.
