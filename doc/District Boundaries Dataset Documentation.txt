* **object_id**: Unique numeric identifier for the dataset row.
* **regname**: Specific neighborhood or statistical sub-zone name (ж.к. Люлин - център).
* **rajon**: Administrative district of Sofia (Люлин).
* **area_sos500**: Area in square meters likely related to specific municipal zoning, public service infrastructure (Система за обслужване), or a 500m buffer zone.
* **rzp_jilsgr**: Total Built-up Area (Разгъната застроена площ - РЗП) specifically for residential buildings (жилищни сгради), measured in square meters.
* **rzp_all**: Total Built-up Area (РЗП) for all buildings in the zone, regardless of purpose.
* **redukcia_20%**: The net usable indoor area. This is calculated by taking `rzp_all` and subtracting 20% to account for shared spaces (stairwells, corridors) and physical walls.
* **ppl_30kvm**: Estimated population capacity of the zone if every resident occupies exactly 30 sq.m. of the net usable area (`redukcia_20%` / 30).
* **ppl_35kvm**: Estimated population capacity based on an allowance of 35 sq.m. per person.
* **ppl_40kvm**: Estimated population capacity based on an allowance of 40 sq.m. per person.
* **area_ge**: The gross physical area of the zone in square meters, used as the baseline denominator for the density calculations.
* **dens30_ppl_kvkm**: Population density expressed as people per square kilometer, based on the 30 sq.m. per person population estimate.
* **dens35_ppl_kvkm**: Population density based on the 35 sq.m. per person estimate.
* **dens40_ppl_kvkm**: Population density based on the 40 sq.m. per person estimate.
* **id**: A unique alphanumeric identifier (UUID) for the geospatial feature.
* **area_kv_m**: The exact algorithmically calculated physical area of the polygon geometry in square meters (closely matching `area_ge`).
* **geometry**: The spatial coordinates defining the physical boundaries of the zone for GIS mapping.
